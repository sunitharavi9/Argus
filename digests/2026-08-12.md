### JigShape: Evaluating Visual-Geometric Reasoning in VLMs through Jigsaw Puzzles
**Authors:** Unknown | **Published:** 2026-08-12

**Core Problem:** This paper addresses the lack of strong geometric reasoning in zero-shot visual-language models (VLMs).

**Approach:**
- Introduces a benchmark with tab-and-blank interlocking pieces that provide strong local compatibility requirements.
- Evaluates VLMs on 95K instances at four grid densities (4$\times$4 to 16$\times$16).
- Compares the performance of zero-shot VLMs with supervised fine-tuning.

**Results:**
- Finds that zero-shot VLMs largely lack geometric reasoning, with only one model exceeding random baseline on 4$\times$4 puzzles.
- Shows that supervised fine-tuning achieves high accuracy, but all models collapse on larger puzzle sizes.
- Highlights the need for improved geometric reasoning in VLMs.
