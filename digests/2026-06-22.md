**Authors:** Sihui Dai, Mann Patel | **Published:** 2026-06-18

**Approach:**

- Evaluates four models and analyzes the impact of demonstration ordering and preference optimization on harmful compliance.

**Results:**
- Benign and harmful demonstrations are not interchangeable, and their effect on harmful compliance depends on the model.
