### [Inherited Circuits, Learned Semantics: How Fine-Tuning Creates Evasion Vulnerabilities Invisible to Standard Evaluation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.27091v1)
**Authors:** Ryan Fetterman | **Published:** 2026-06-25
**Core Problem:** Fine-tuning large language models can introduce evasion vulnerabilities that are not detected by standard evaluation methods.
**Approach:**
- Investigated the impact of fine-tuning on model vulnerabilities using Foundation-Sec-8B-Instruct and its base model, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct.
- Analyzed the classification circuit to understand how fine-tuning affects model semantics.
- Used causal interventions to localize the classification circuit and understand its behavior.
**Results:**
- Fine-tuning concentrates and semantically specializes the classification circuit, making it more vulnerable to evasion attacks.
- The inherited circuit from the base model is more important than the fine-tuning process in determining the model's vulnerabilities.
- The study highlights the need for more comprehensive evaluation methods to detect evasion vulnerabilities.
