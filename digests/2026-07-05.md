**Authors:** Josh Hills, Ida Caspary, Asa Cooper Stickland | **Published:** 2026-07-02

**Core Problem:** This paper addresses the gap in understanding how AI coding agents can be vulnerable to distributed attacks across multiple sessions due to their persistent state.

**Approach:**
- Introduced Iterative VibeCoding, a setting for AI control to study safely deploying capable but potentially untrusted AI.

**Results:**

- Showed the effectiveness of Iterative VibeCoding as a framework for studying AI control and safety.
