**Approach:**

### [SocietyBench: Forecasting Counterfactual Social-World Evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04009v1)
**Authors:** Zhenran Wang, Zhonghan Bian, Jinsong Li | **Published:** 2026-08-04
**Core Problem:** This paper introduces SocietyBench to measure a model's ability to forecast how real social events unfold.
**Approach:**
- Collects Web news and social-media posts across five platforms for a given event topic.
- Distills these into a date-indexed timeline separating factual events and public opinion.
- Turns every cutoff date into an audited bank of forecasting questions.
**Results:**
- Provides a benchmark for evaluating a model's social forecasting ability on two axes: probability calibration and temporal accuracy.
- Offers a complementary social ability to task completion benchmarks for large language models.

### [WorldCup Arena: Prospective, Leakage-Free Evaluation of Frontier LLMs on a Live Tournament](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04008v1)
**Authors:** Zhenran Wang, Zhonghan Bian, Jinsong Li | **Published:** 2026-08-04
**Core Problem:** This paper presents WorldCup Arena, a prospective and leakage-free evaluation of frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) on a live tournament setting.
**Approach:**
- Utilizes the 2026 FIFA World Cup to evaluate LLMs' forecasting ability in a real-world scenario.
- Asks LLMs to fill in prediction cards for matches before kickoffs, ensuring a leakage-free evaluation.
**Results:**
- Establishes a set of behaviors that can be used to evaluate LLMs in a live, real-world setting.
- Provides insights into the forecasting capabilities of frontier LLMs in dynamic environments.
