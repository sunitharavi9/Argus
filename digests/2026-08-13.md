**Results:**

### [Redistribution-based Cost Inference Improves Sparse Safe Offline RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12306v1)
**Authors:** Ebenezer Gelo, Geraud Nangue Tasse, Steven James | **Published:** 2026-08-12

**Core Problem:** The paper targets the challenge of sparse cost annotations in safe offline reinforcement learning.

**Approach:**
- Introduced the Redistribution-based Cost Inference (RCI) framework.
- RCI converts sparse stop-feedback into dense per-step costs via return decomposition.
- It trains a constrained offline policy on the augmented dataset.

**Results:**
- RCI preserves the feasible policy set and the optimal Lagrangian in a CMDP.
- The framework improves the conditioning of cost critic learning.
- Experiments on highway driving and robotic manipulation demonstrate its effectiveness.
