### Reading Task Failure Off the Activations: A Sparse-Feature Audit of GPT-2 Small on Indirect Object Identification
**Authors:** Mahdi Nasermoghadasi | **Published:** 2026-05-21

**Core Problem:** This paper addresses the gap in understanding what causes GPT-2 Small to fail on the Indirect Object Identification task.

**Approach:**
- Used a sparse-autoencoder (SAE) to identify features that fire differently on failed versus successful trials.

**Results:**
