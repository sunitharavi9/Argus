### [Calibrating Conservatism for Scalable Oversight](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28807v1)
**Authors:** William Overman, Mohsen Bayati | **Published:** 2026-05-27
**Core Problem:** Existing approaches to scalable oversight of agentic AI systems rely on complex assumptions or lack practical methods for sequential settings with statistical guarantees.
**Approach:**
- Introduced Calibrated Collective Oversight (CCO), which aggregates diverse auxiliary scoring functions into a penalty measuring deviation from a conservative baseline.
- Inspired by Attainable Utility Preservation, CCO enables collective conservatism.
- Actions face a penalty proportional to overseer concern.
**Results:**
- High-utility actions are still selected when overseers find them unobjectionable.
- CCO provides a framework for scalable oversight of agentic AI systems.
- The approach has potential limitations in terms of overseer bias and scoring function selection.
