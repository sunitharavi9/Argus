**Authors:** Yifan Jiang, Ruoxi Ning, Sheng Yao | **Published:** 2026-05-26

**Approach:**

- Employed probing and canonical correlation analysis to examine model representations

**Results:**

- Limitations of current vision-language models in handling visual evidence are highlighted

### LocateAnything: Fast and High-Quality Vision-Language Grounding with Parallel Box Decoding
**Authors:** Shihao Wang, Shilong Liu, Yuanguo Kuang | **Published:** 2026-05-26

**Core Problem:** Vision-language models often struggle with serializing 2D boxes into multiple 1D tokens, leading to decoding bottlenecks.

**Approach:**
- Introduced Parallel Box Decoding (PBD) for unified generative grounding and detection
- Decoded geometric elements as atomic units in a single step
- Developed a scalable framework for efficient decoding

**Results:**
- Improved decoding throughput and localization accuracy
- PBD preserves intra-box geometric coherence and unlocks substantial parallelism
- Limitations of existing decoding approaches are addressed through PBD
