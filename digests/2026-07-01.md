### [Harnessing Textual Refusal Directions for Multimodal Safety](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31876v1)
**Authors:** Moreno D'Incà, Massimiliano Mancini, Nicu Sebe | **Published:** 2026-06-30

**Core Problem:** This paper addresses the challenge of improving safety in Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) by exploring the use of textual refusal directions.

**Approach:**
- The authors investigate whether textual refusal directions can generalize across modalities, including image and video.
- They introduce Modality-Agnostic Refusal Steering (MARS) as a method to steer safe multimodal inputs toward refusal directions.
- The effectiveness of MARS is conditioned by layer selection, steering strength, and cross-modal alignment.

**Results:**
- Preliminary findings confirm the ability of textual refusal directions to generalize across modalities.
- The effectiveness of MARS is demonstrated, although it is affected by cross-modal alignment, which can cause safe inputs to be spuriously steered toward refusal.
- The results highlight the potential of MARS for improving multimodal safety but also note the need for further refinement.
