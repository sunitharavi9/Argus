### [RiskFlow: Fast and Faithful Safety-Critical Traffic Scenario Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06423v1)
**Authors:** Qi Lan, Yining Tang, Yu Shen | **Published:** 2026-06-04

**Core Problem:** Existing diffusion-based methods for safety-critical traffic scenario generation are computationally expensive and may produce unrealistic motion artifacts.

**Approach:**
- RiskFlow formulates future trajectory generation as transport in the action space.
- It learns an average velocity field over a finite interval to transform Gaussian action sequences.
- The framework is designed for closed-loop safety-critical multi-agent traffic generation.

**Results:**
- RiskFlow achieves fast and faithful generation of safety-critical traffic scenarios.
- It reduces the computational cost compared to iterative denoising methods.
- The approach improves the realism of generated scenarios by minimizing motion artifacts.
