### [Toward a Gricean Retreat: Probing LLMs for Knowledge Boundaries and Referent Specificity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.13484v1)
**Authors:** Dananjay Srinivas, Saksham Khatwani, Maria Pacheco | **Published:** 2026-08-13
**Core Problem:** LLMs often fabricate plausible-sounding details when asked about entities outside their knowledge boundary.
**Approach:**
- Framed the failure through a Gricean lens: a cooperative speaker retreats up the specificity hierarchy when uncertain
- Probed LLMs to answer two questions: (i) do their activations encode whether a referent falls inside the knowledge boundary, and (ii) do they anticipate the specificity of the referent they are about to generate?
**Results:**
- Found that LLMs' activations encode knowledge boundary information, but do not reconcile this with referent specificity
- Highlighted the importance of improving LLMs' ability to recognize and respond to knowledge boundaries
- Identified areas for future research in developing more effective Gricean retreat strategies for LLMs
