### [ALIGNBEAM : Inference-Time Alignment Transfer via Cross-Vocabulary Logit Mixing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12342v1)
**Authors:** Chirag Chawla, Pratinav Seth, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu | **Published:** 2026-06-10

**Core Problem:** Domain fine-tuning of large language models can degrade their safety by making them more susceptible to harmful prompts.

**Approach:**
- Proposed a training-free method called ALIGNBEAM that lifts the restriction of requiring shared vocabulary between models.
- Developed a token-by-token translation of anchor logits into the target model's vocabulary.
- Utilized a small LLM judge to select the safest among candidate continuations.

**Results:**
- Demonstrated that ALIGNBEAM substantially improves the safety of large language models.
- Showed that ALIGNBEAM can be used across both cross-vocabulary and same-vocabulary evaluation pairs.
- Highlighted the potential of ALIGNBEAM for improving the safety-utility trade-off in LLMs.
