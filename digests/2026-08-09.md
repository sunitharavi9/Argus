### [An Optimal Agnostic PAC Algorithm](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06363v1)
**Authors:** Markus Engelund Mathiasen, Jian Qian, Nikita Zhivotovskiy | **Published:** 2026-08-06

**Core Problem:** Achieving statistically optimal risk bounds in agnostic PAC learning, which is crucial for the reliable deployment of machine learning models in uncertain environments.

**Approach:**
- The authors construct a learner that achieves optimal risk bounds from an i.i.d. sample.
- The algorithm is designed to work with classes of finite VC dimension, providing a general framework for learning.
- The approach focuses on minimizing the gap between the learned hypothesis and the best possible hypothesis in the class.

**Results:**
- The algorithm achieves optimal risk bounds that match lower bounds up to universal constants.
- The results are significant for understanding the sample complexity of agnostic PAC learning.
- The limitation to classes of finite VC dimension is a constraint, but the work provides a foundation for further research.

### [MASS: Multiplayer World Models with Authoritative Shared State](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06257)
**Authors:** Unknown | **Published:** 2026-08-09

**Core Problem:** Current video world models struggle with multiplayer environments due to entanglement of world state with view-dependent visual latents.

**Approach:**
- The authors propose MASS, which disentangles world dynamics and view rendering.
- A learned Logic Engine advances a global, authoritative typed state, acting as the sole recurrent memory and synchronization reference.
- A learned Rendering Engine generates independent and consistent views for any requested camera on demand.

**Results:**
- MASS achieves superior state estimation and view rendering capabilities.
- The disentangling approach allows for better scalability and reduced redundant compute.
- The work has implications for more realistic and efficient modeling of complex, multiplayer environments.

**Approach:**
- The authors compare programmatic tool calling to native JSON tool calling across 14 language models.

### [The Low Frequency Trap: Video Language Models Fail at Simple Event Bookkeeping](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06361)
**Authors:** Sarvesh Baskar, Zikui Cai, Shayan Shabihi | **Published:** 2026-08-06

**Core Problem:** Video language models' inability to accurately count events in videos, particularly at low frequencies.

**Approach:**
- The authors introduce trace-grounded parametric profiling for event counting in controlled video tasks.
- Event count N and frequency F are varied while holding rendering fixed.
- Executable event traces are used for capability-surface estimation and timestamp-level evaluation.

**Results:**
- The models exhibit a staged temporal failure, with performance degrading at low event frequencies.
- The study highlights the need for better temporal modeling and event counting capabilities in video language models.
- The approach provides a novel framework for evaluating and improving model performance in this area.
