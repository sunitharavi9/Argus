### [When Words Are Safe But Actions Kill: Probing Physical Danger Beyond Text Safety in Hidden-State Risk Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15218v1)
**Authors:** Weimeng Wang, Ziqiang Wang, Zihang Zhan | **Published:** 2026-07-16

**Core Problem:** This paper addresses the gap between text-level content danger and physically grounded danger in large language models.

**Approach:**
- Hidden-state direction analysis is used to study the representations of language models.
- Random-split null tests are employed to validate the findings.
- A logistic probe called PRISM is proposed to detect physical danger.

**Results:**
- The paper shows that content danger and physical danger form separable signals in language model representations.
- PRISM achieves high accuracy (86.2-87.7%) on a safety benchmark with a low false positive rate (11.7-13.7%).
- The results outperform same-scale language model judges, which have a higher false positive rate (24.7-39.0%).
