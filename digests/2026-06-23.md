**Approach:**
- Developed a parametric safety benchmark to generate safety-critical scenarios with comprehensive stochasticity.
- Created a novel keypose-driven data generation pipeline to overcome scalability bottlenecks.
- Curated a large-scale dataset of 19,664 strictly collision-free demonstrations with extensive domain randomization.

**Results:**

- The benchmark provides a systematic evaluation of VLA and embodied foundation models.
