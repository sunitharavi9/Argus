### [When Words Are Safe But Actions Kill: Probing Physical Danger Beyond Text Safety in Hidden-State Risk Space](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15218v1)
**Authors:** Weimeng Wang, Ziqiang Wang, Zihang Zhan | **Published:** 2026-07-16

**Core Problem:** Large language models (LLMs) may generate linguistically benign instructions that become unsafe when grounded in the physical world.

**Approach:**
- Studied whether physically grounded danger is the same safety problem as ordinary text-level content danger.
- Used hidden-state direction analysis and random-split null tests to show that content danger and physical danger form separable signals in LLM representations.
- Proposed PRISM, a single-layer L2-regularized logistic probe over full hidden states to detect physical danger.

**Results:**
- Content danger and physical danger are separable signals in LLM representations.
- PRISM achieves high accuracy on SafeAgentBench with a low false positive rate.
- The results highlight the need to consider physical danger when evaluating the safety of LLMs.
