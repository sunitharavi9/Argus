### [The Illusion of Equivalency: Statistical Characterization of Quantization Effects in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08734v1)
**Authors:** Baha Rababah, Cuneyt Gurcan Akcora, Carson K. Leung | **Published:** 2026-07-09
**Core Problem:** Post-training quantization of large language models (LLMs) can lead to behavioral changes that are not captured by traditional evaluation metrics like accuracy and perplexity.
**Approach:**
- Introduced correctness agreement, a metric to measure the overlap in correct predictions between a base model and its quantized variants.
- Analyzed quantization effects across multiple models and quantization schemes.
- Examined layer-wise distortions using statistical and distributional metrics.
**Results:**
- Found that quantization can induce significant behavioral divergence in LLMs, even when task performance appears preserved.
- Showed that correctness agreement and layer-wise distortions provide valuable insights into the effects of quantization.
- Highlighted the importance of considering these effects in the deployment of quantized LLMs.

**Results:**
