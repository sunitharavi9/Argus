**Authors:** Chenrui Fan, Paolo Favaro | **Published:** 2026-06-23

**Core Problem:** This paper addresses the gap in evaluating the 3D consistency of text-to-video (T2V) models, which is crucial for ensuring the generated frames provide coherent multi-view evidence for a single static 3D scene.

**Approach:**
- The authors propose a reconstruction-based diagnostic benchmark called GeoT2V-Bench.
- They estimate per-frame camera intrinsics and poses using VGGT-style geometry estimation and fit DeformableGS.
- The pipeline derives a static MedianGS proxy by temporal-median aggregation and renders this proxy along the estimated camera path.

**Results:**
- GeoT2V-Bench evaluates whether camera-prompted T2V clips can support explicit rigid 3D reconstruction.
- The benchmark provides a more comprehensive evaluation of T2V models beyond visual plausibility.
- The approach can help identify limitations in current T2V models and guide future improvements.
